Employee Operations & Compliance Guide

Standard Operating Procedures (SOP), Maker-Checker guidelines, and version control features

1. Maker-Checker Dual Control Policy

BankVCS 2.0 strictly enforces Dual Control (Maker-Checker policy) on high-impact administrative and version control actions.

  • Self-Approval Prohibition: A staff member who submits a rollback or version restoration request (Maker) CANNOT approve their own request (Checker).
  • Audit Trail: Every submission, approval, and rejection is recorded in the immutable audit log with staff user IDs and timestamps.
  • Queue Review: Check the Approvals Queue regularly to process requests submitted by peer staff members.
2. Entity Version Control & Rollbacks

All entity modifications (Customers, Accounts, Beneficiaries) create immutable `EntityVersion` records.

  • Forward-Only Rollbacks: Reverting an entity to a prior state never deletes history; it appends a new forward version snapshot.
  • Non-Financial Entities: Customer profiles, beneficiaries, and preferences can be restored via the version control view.
  • Diff Inspection: Use the Version Diff tool to compare changes line-by-line before requesting rollbacks.
3. Risk Scoring & Fraud Monitoring

Automated risk rules evaluate transactions and accounts in real-time.

  • High-Value Transactions: Transactions exceeding $10,000 generate automatic compliance alerts.
  • Risk Thresholds: Customers with a risk score above 70 are flagged for staff review.
  • Account Freezing: If fraud is suspected, contact compliance admins or freeze account access via the customer profile.
4. Customer Support & Complaints Handling

Manage customer inquiries through the integrated ticketing workflow.

  • Ticket Lifecycle: OPEN → IN_PROGRESS → RESOLVED → CLOSED.
  • Resolution Notes: Always add detailed investigation notes before marking tickets as RESOLVED.
